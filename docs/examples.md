## A real run

Shortened and paraphrased from one of the author's Codex sessions, with personal details removed.

### A page that fell short at the doctor's

The owner found a family health-information page inconvenient during appointments.

The agent compared what a visit needs (preparing questions, recording answers, knowing what to do afterwards) with what the code does, instead of starting from fonts or card order. It found one possible gap: the "I know the next step" ticks disappeared when the visit view closed, and none of them recorded what the next step was. It also noted that the product is a navigation aid, not a medical record, so the patient's own notes should stay apart from accepted medical conclusions. Its recommendation was to replay one recent difficult appointment and find the moment the page stopped helping, before choosing a redesign.

**What changed:** the next step became checking whether the page supports the visit, not changing its layout.
