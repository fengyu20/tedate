# What Tedate's answers look like

Back to the [README](../README.md).

A good answer opens by naming its mode (advise, delegate or execute), then gives a conclusion and a reason. A small question stops there. Any other answer adds what it did not check and who does what next, and ends with one line naming the decision it recommends (a recommendation until you accept it). Larger decisions get four written answers: what is being decided, how far the evidence goes, what it recommends, and what would change the decision.

Fixed labels used in answers:

- **Adding a feature:** `IMPLEMENT_NOW`, `OBSERVE`, `DEFER` or `NOT_PLANNED`, with the smallest scope and, for all but `IMPLEMENT_NOW`, what would reopen it. Accepted decisions stay in force unless you replace them.
- **Choosing between two options:** the choice, one reason and what would reopen it.
- **"The page looks wrong":** the two or three things that most affect the impression, a comparable task for a design skill, and a recheck of each named problem on the same page.
- **"All checks passed":** whether the original goal is `MET`, `NOT_MET`, `NOT_SHOWN` or `UNVERIFIED`, and what kind of check ran (static, unit, real run).
- **Many things to do, or whether to stop:** one test for ranking, and a stop condition written before the evidence is read.
