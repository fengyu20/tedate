# Listen, then reach

This skill judges and prepares; the owner contacts people.

## Listen (before building, and again whenever reach is the question)

If a research skill is installed (for example customer-research or last30days), hand the searching to it and judge what comes back; first check whether it sends the idea to third-party services, and let the owner decide. Otherwise search public sources by hand: where people talk about this problem, the words they use, the related queries search engines suggest. Three rules either way:

- **Grade every finding by what the source is.** High: several independent sources agree and record what people actually did (switched, paid, built a workaround). Medium: one source of recorded behaviour, or people describing their own past behaviour. Low: stated interest or opinion. Being prompted by a question lowers a source's weight but does not void it, and being unprompted does not make it reliable; a count of sources alone never makes it high. Look for evidence against the idea too.
- **Say versus do.** What people say is a lead; what they do (switch, pay, build a workaround, search) is evidence.
- **Name what you could not see.** Sources behind a login or a limit, and any source that failed, are reported as unseen, not as quiet. When the evidence is thin, say so.

## Reach (the product is live and has few or no visitors)

1. **Check the numbers.** Are visits real (not your own previews, not bots)? Where did the real ones come from?
2. **Readiness.** Fix known blockers to the core task before inviting people. If user value is still uncertain, run a small reach test and use the result to update the hypothesis, not as a definitive answer about demand.
3. **Listen** as above, if it has not been done.
4. **Pick one.** One group of people you can reach, one sentence for them, one channel.
5. **Test small.** Write the success signal first (one number or behaviour) and the time to look. Prepare the post or message for the owner to send.
6. **Read the result.** People acting (visiting, returning, asking, paying) is strong evidence; likes and replies are weak. Decide: continue, change group, message or channel, or stop. With few visits the result picks the next check; it does not settle demand either way.
